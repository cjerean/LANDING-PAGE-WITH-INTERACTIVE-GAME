Our Story

History of The Business

Born from the ocean depths, Deep Divers Diner has been serving legendary meals to sea-worn travelers and thrill-seekers alike since 1998.

1998

The First Dive

Captain Morgan founded the diner inside a refurbished submarine hull on the edge of the Mariana Trench.

2005

Discovering the Kraken

Our head chef introduced the legendary Kraken BBQ, cementing our status in the culinary world.

2024

Going Global

We expanded our reach to land-dwellers, bringing deep-sea delicacies to major ports worldwide.
