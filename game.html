🌊 How to Dive

🕹️
Move

WASD or Arrow Keys to swim in any direction. Swipe on mobile.

🐟
Eat Food

Swim into small glowing fish, shrimp, and crabs to score points.

🪼
Avoid Danger

Steer clear of jellyfish and large predator fish — they'll cost you a life!

⬆️
Level Up

Every 8 creatures eaten bumps up the level — speed and spawn rate increase!

❤️
Lives

You have 3 lives. After each hit you get a brief moment of invincibility.

⏸
Pause

Press SPACE or ESC to pause anytime. Click buttons above to control the game.
